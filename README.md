## 📌 Project Overview

**FloodIntel** is an AI and GIS-powered web-based flood prediction and damage risk
assessment system developed as a **Final Year Project (FYP)** at **SLTC Research University**.

The system combines **Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, GIS, spatial analysis,
database technologies, and web-based dashboards** to provide a data-driven approach
to understanding flood conditions and their potential impact.

The project focuses on the **Lower Kelani River Basin in Sri Lanka**, particularly
the areas of:

- Hanwella
- Kaduwela
- Kolonnawa

FloodIntel processes environmental and spatial information, applies trained AI models
for water-level forecasting and flood severity classification, and presents the
results through interactive dashboards and GIS-based visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of FloodIntel are to:

- Develop an AI-based approach for forecasting river water levels.
- Classify potential flood severity using deep learning.
- Visualize flood risk using GIS and spatial data.
- Assess potential building damage based on flood-related conditions.
- Provide interactive dashboards for monitoring flood risk.
- Support identification of high-risk areas.
- Provide alerts and warnings based on identified risk levels.
- Generate analytical reports for decision support.
- Demonstrate how AI and GIS can support flood preparedness and disaster risk
  assessment in Sri Lanka.
